# Engineering Build Log

## 2026-11-19 — Project Kickoff & Architecture Selection
- **Goal:** Build a cute, two-hemisphere rolling companion robot with blinking LED eyes and future camera tracking.
- **Mechanical Architecture:** Selected a split-shell differential drive (left and right rotating domes driven by independent motors) around a stationary weighted center ring.
- **Why this design:** Keeps the eyes and future camera upright and unobstructed without looking through scuffed, rotating plastic, and guarantees zero wires cross a rotating joint.
